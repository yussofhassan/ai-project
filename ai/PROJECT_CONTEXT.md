# Quran Academy Platform

# Project Context

Version: 1.0

Status: Active

Last Updated: July 2026

---

# Overview

Quran Academy Platform is a production-grade Software as a Service (SaaS) platform designed to manage Quran academies.

The platform is not an e-learning website.

The platform is not a Learning Management System (LMS).

The platform is an Academy Operating System (AOS).

Its primary purpose is to digitize every operational process inside a Quran academy.

---

# Why This Project Exists

Many Quran academies begin as a single teacher working independently.

As the academy grows, operations become increasingly difficult to manage.

Common challenges include:

- Student management
- Parent communication
- Payment tracking
- Attendance recording
- Homework management
- Teacher assignment
- Group management
- Scheduling
- Reporting

The platform centralizes all of these processes into one integrated system.

---

# Core Philosophy

The software assists humans.

The software never replaces humans.

Teachers teach.

Administrators manage.

The platform organizes.

---

# Product Vision

The system should become the operating system for Quran academies.

Every daily academy operation should eventually be performed inside this platform.

---

# Academy Structure

The academy consists of:

- Students
- Parents
- Teachers
- Academic Administrators
- Payment Administrators
- Super Administrators

These users collaborate through one integrated platform.

---

# Academic Philosophy

Academic decisions are always made by humans.

Examples include:

- Creating groups
- Assigning teachers
- Scheduling placement tests
- Student transfers
- Level adjustments

The software provides information and tools.

Administrators make the final decision.

---

# Parent Experience

Parents may have multiple children.

Parents receive notifications about every important event affecting their children.

Parents may switch between their children's dashboards without logging in again.

---

# Student Experience

Students have independent dashboards.

Students can:

- View lessons
- Join Google Meet sessions
- View homework
- View attendance
- View grades
- Renew subscriptions

Students never choose teachers or groups.

---

# Teacher Experience

Teachers focus on teaching.

Teachers do not manage academy operations.

Teachers can:

- Conduct lessons
- Record attendance
- Publish homework
- Record memorization grades
- Record revision grades
- Write lesson notes

---

# Administrator Experience

Administrators coordinate academy operations.

Responsibilities include:

- Managing groups
- Assigning teachers
- Assigning students
- Scheduling placement tests
- Reviewing academic operations

Academic decisions are intentionally manual.

---

# Financial Operations

Payments require manual approval.

No payment activates subscriptions automatically.

Payment administrators verify every submitted payment.

---

# Notifications

The platform communicates through notifications.

Notifications are generated by business events.

Examples:

- Payment approved
- Payment rejected
- Homework assigned
- Lesson cancelled
- Group assigned
- Placement test scheduled

---

# Long-Term Vision

The platform should eventually support:

- Multiple academies
- Multiple branches
- Mobile applications
- WhatsApp integration
- SMS integration
- Advanced analytics
- AI-assisted administrative tools

AI may assist administrators.

AI never replaces administrators.
