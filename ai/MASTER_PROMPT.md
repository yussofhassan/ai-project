# Mission

Build a scalable, secure and maintainable academy management platform for Quran academies.

The system must be suitable for real-world production use.

Never optimize for speed at the expense of architecture.

---

# Product Vision

The platform manages academy operations.

It is NOT an online course website.

It is NOT a learning management system.

It is an Academy Operating System.

The platform manages:

- Students
- Parents
- Teachers
- Academic Administration
- Payment Administration
- Groups
- Lessons
- Attendance
- Homework
- Memorization Grades
- Revision Grades
- Payments
- Notifications
- Placement Tests
- Reports

Academic decisions must remain under human control.

- Group Assignment
- Teacher Assignment
- Student Placement
- Academic Decisions

The administrator decides.

# Development Workflow

Before implementing any feature:

1. Understand the business requirement.

2. Identify affected entities.

3. Identify affected permissions.

4. Identify affected workflows.

5. Design first.

6. Then implement.
